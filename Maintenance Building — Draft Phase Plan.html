How the recommended loads below were set

  • Baseline = the heaviest weight you've actually hit on that lift (not last session — several exercises show a lighter "recent" number because weeks 9–10 were the Peak phase's lighter pump-work sets).
  • Week 11 start = baseline + 2.5 lb, i.e. "at that or slightly higher," per your instruction.
  • Progression: Bench Press and Barbell Squat (your two barbell anchors) climb +2.5 lb every week. Everything else climbs +2.5 lb every 2 weeks — enough overload to keep building without turning a maintenance block into a second peak.
  • Core (Hanging Knees to Chest, Body Saw Plank, L Sits, Weight Plate Russian Twist) is bodyweight, placed last in each session, with reps/hold time creeping up every 2 weeks.
  • Chin Ups & Pull Ups are new additions — you did your first unassisted rep today (not logged yet), so both start at 1–2 reps × 3 sets and build toward 4–5 by week 16. Chin Ups sit in Upper Body A, Pull Ups in Upper Body B / Full Body.
  • Warm-up opens every session with a 10-minute full-body foam roll, then the band/mobility work already in rotation. Cool-down stretches carry over unchanged from weeks 9–10.

  • Exercise picks — day templates are built from what you actually did most recently (weeks 6–9: bench/incline/rows for Upper A, squat/RDL/leg press for Lower A, Arnold press/decline/rear-pulldown for Upper B). Swap anything that's not working for you.
  • Full Body placement — I put them at week 13 Day 3 and week 16 Day 3 (end of each 3-week block). Fine, or would you rather they replace a different day / land elsewhere?
  • Progression rate — +2.5 lb/week on bench & squat means squat goes 82.5 → 95 lb and bench 77.5 → 90 lb over the 6 weeks. That roughly matches your actual pace in weeks 6–9. Too aggressive, too easy, or right?
  • Chin Up / Pull Up progression — 1–2 reps → 4–5 reps over 6 weeks, 3 sets each time. If a rep is missed some weeks, fine to hold flat rather than push the number — just flag it and I'll adjust.
  • App changes — once the numbers look right I'll: add a phase_group field to the plan model (backend + frontend), seed these 18 sessions into Firestore, put "Reintroduction" / "Maintenance Building" as a title on the Workouts tab, and add a segmented toggle for phase group on the Progress tab.
