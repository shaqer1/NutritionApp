Phase guidance
  • Weeks 17–18: work at 2–3 reps in reserve (RIR), adding reps within each exercise's range.
  • Weeks 19 and 21: increase the load only after every set reaches the top of its rep range with at least 2 RIR; otherwise repeat the load.
  • Week 20: hold Week 19 loads and build reps. Week 22: deload with 2 sets per exercise, about 10% less load, and 3–4 RIR.
  • Rest 2–3 minutes on the main lifts and 1–2 minutes on accessories. Keep at least a day between lower sessions; do not train through pain.

Progression and recovery

Warm-up and ramp-up sets are not included in the working-set counts below
  • Schedule: Day 1 Upper Body A, Day 2 Lower Body A, Day 3 Upper Body B, Day 4 Lower Body B. Place rest days where they fit; try to leave at least one day between lower sessions.
  • Before each session: do the catalog-linked warm-up below, then 2–4 gradual ramp-up sets before the first main lift. Ramp-up sets are not working sets.
  • Weeks 17–18: use the displayed loads; stop each working set with about 2–3 reps in reserve (RIR), and build reps inside the range.
  • Weeks 19 and 21: the planned load rises only if every set reached the top of its rep range with at least 2 RIR. Otherwise repeat the previous load and keep building reps.
  • Week 20: hold the Week 19 load and add reps. Week 22: deload with 2 working sets per lift, about 10% less than Week 21, and 3–4 RIR.
  • Rest 2–3 minutes on squat, deadlift, bench, and rows; 1–2 minutes on accessories. Do not test maxes in this block. Stop or modify a movement if it causes pain.
