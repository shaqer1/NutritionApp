Phase guidance
  • Start at the displayed database-referenced load only if warm-ups feel controlled and leave about 2–3 reps in reserve (RIR); otherwise reduce the load that day.
  • Build reps within the range first. Add about 2.5 lb in Weeks 19 and 21 only after all working sets reach the top of the range with at least 2 RIR; if not, repeat the prior load.
  • Week 20 holds Week 19 loads. Week 22 is a deload: use the Week 17 baseline and 2 working sets per strength exercise at 3–4 RIR.
  • Rest 2–3 minutes for main lifts and 1–2 minutes for accessories; leave at least one day between lower sessions where practical. Do not train through pain.

Progression and recovery

Warm-up and cool-down work is separate from strength exercise counts
  • Day order: Day 1 Upper Body A, Day 2 Lower Body A, Day 3 Upper Body B, Day 4 Lower Body B. Place rest days where they fit and keep at least a day between lower sessions when possible.
  • Before lifting: complete the listed mobility warm-up, then use gradual ramp-up sets before the first main lift; these are not working sets.
  • Weeks 17–18: hold the database-referenced baseline and build reps at 2–3 RIR. Weeks 19 and 21: the suggested +2.5 lb step is earned, not automatic. Week 20: hold and build reps.
  • Week 22: return to baseline loads, reduce to 2 working sets per strength movement, and keep 3–4 RIR. Core remains last in each day's strength list.
  • Use the closest available equipment increment, and reduce a target if technique, pain, or warm-up performance calls for it. No max testing in this block.
